# Alt text

Two-panel figure comparing effective-cost change for 45 fixed cheetah core pairs under low, balanced, and high vegetation-resistance assumptions. Panel A shows median and interquartile change for 2012–2016, 2016–2020, 2020–2024, and 2012–2024. Median costs declined in the first two intervals and increased from 2020 to 2024 under all three assumptions. Panel B shows the link-level 2012–2024 distributions; increasing vegetation weight widens the magnitude of both positive and negative changes. Forty-three of 45 links retained the same direction across all three scenarios.
